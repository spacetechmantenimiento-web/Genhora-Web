# Recursos fotográficos

Generados para este proyecto con **Imagegen integrado**, no con CLI ni API keys del usuario. No son fotografías de pedidos, clientes o inventario real. No contienen marcas de terceros ni lettering generado. Los archivos originales se conservaron y las versiones web solo cambian formato/compresión a WebP, calidad 86.

Su resolución efectiva es de unos 396 px por celda; sustituir por fotografías individuales cuando existan. Las imágenes generadas no certifican derechos exclusivos de marca ni disponibilidad de los objetos mostrados.

One small sage paper card rests nearby. Organic sculptural arrangement, tasteful creative fashion still life, close crop with products filling frame. Seamless pale warm beige studio backdrop, soft sunlight coming from upper left, real subtle shadows, visible woven cotton, ceramic glaze and matte limestone textures. Refined serene warm palette cream #F9F7F0, sage #ADC8B3, deep forest #294D3A. No people, no plants, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks. All products totally unbranded. This is an illustrative concept, not actual client work.

### Catálogo

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: product photographic contact sheet for an editorial ecommerce catalog, will be displayed in individual cells. Create a perfectly regular FIVE COLUMNS by TWO ROWS grid, exactly TEN square tiles, final aspect ratio 5:2 wide, high resolution 3840x1536. No gutters, no lines, no borders. Each cell has the SAME seamless pale warm beige background and soft sunlight shadow. Each contains exactly one centered product well within cell boundaries with generous space, identical catalog photo camera angle and scale. TOP ROW from left to right: (1) plain ivory short-sleeve cotton T-shirt neatly laid flat (2) plain sage green folded sweatshirt with sleeves visible (3) plain natural canvas tote bag with handles (4) plain ivory ceramic mug (5) plain forest green stainless steel travel tumbler. BOTTOM ROW from left to right: (6) cream blank invitation card with matching sage envelope (7) three blank sage and ivory round stickers gently overlapping (8) natural beige blank round keychain with brass ring (9) blank forest green cloth notebook (10) simple corporate gift set of blank ivory box, sage notebook and brass pen. Premium realistic studio product photography with cotton ceramic paper metal textures. Strictly no text or letters or brands, no watermark, no graphics. Precise uniform 5x2 grid layout. Each product completely separated into its own square tile. Natural warm shadows, elegant editorial still life.

La resolución real devuelta por Imagegen fue 1983 × 793; no se presenta la resolución pedida en el prompt como resolución efectiva.
